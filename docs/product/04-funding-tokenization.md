# 04 — Funding and Future Tokenization

## Funding Hub

Funding Hub supports transparent missions, not vague donations.

Campaign fields:

- title;
- type;
- goal amount;
- raised amount;
- use of funds;
- milestones;
- updates;
- reward tiers;
- refund policy;
- status;
- participant list visibility.

## Campaign Types

| Type | Allowed MVP? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Support mission | yes | clear perks |
| Preorder | yes | deliver product/access |
| Founder Pass | yes | benefits and status |
| Donation | maybe | avoid default framing |
| Investment | no | legal structure required |
| Revenue share | no | legal review required |

## Safe Copy

Use:

- support this mission;
- back development;
- unlock access;
- receive benefits;
- contribution history.

Avoid:

- invest;
- equity;
- passive income;
- dividends;
- guaranteed token;
- guaranteed return.

## Future Digital Economy

Candidates:

- Utility Credits;
- Contribution Reputation;
- Access Token;
- Compute-backed token;
- Ecosystem Token;
- Project launch assets.

Before any token:

- real product demand;
- clear utility;
- legal review;
- tax review;
- anti-speculation messaging;
- historical contribution data preserved.

Data to preserve now:

- contributions;
- reputation events;
- benefits granted;
- funding participation;
- referral conversions;
- product usage;
- mission history;
- season participation.
